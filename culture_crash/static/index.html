    The engine scans everyday life for five tells: new slang, workarounds, over-intense micro-communities, low-grade shame and rising friction. It clusters them into spaces of unnamed need and scores each on cultural distance × need intensity. It then dives into far, randomly seeded domains for a real concept with the same shape, and collides the two into Oblique Strategy-style cards. Every claim links back to a source.

    Nothing leaves the engine until a person answers one question: does naming this give people something, or take something from them?
